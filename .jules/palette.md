## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA labels on Icon-Only Close Buttons
**Learning:** Found a systemic pattern across the application where various modal and panel close buttons (using '×' or '\u2715' characters) lacked `aria-label` attributes. This prevented screen reader users from understanding the purpose of these interactive elements. The affected buttons were identified by their IDs (`btn-close-diplomacy`, `btn-close-sentinel`, `btn-close-settings`, `btn-help-close`, `btn-mission-toggle`).
**Action:** When creating or reviewing UI components, especially overlays or modals, always ensure that icon-only buttons (like 'close' or 'toggle' buttons) include descriptive `aria-label` attributes (e.g., `aria-label="Close settings"`) to guarantee accessibility for assistive technologies.
